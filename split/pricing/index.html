TaxPower pricing questions

What does the installation price include?

The displayed price is the one-time installation fee. Support and software updates are free for the first year from purchase. 18% GST will be added to the displayed installation price. Optional LAN access is extra.

When does annual AMC apply?

AMC applies after the first year. The annual AMC for your selected products is shown separately from the installation price.

Which edition should I choose?

Professional is intended for CAs, consultants and tax practices; Business is intended for companies and finance teams. Installation and AMC vary by edition. Contact us to confirm the licence that fits your requirements.

Can I use TaxPower on additional LAN computers?

Optional LAN access costs ₹500 plus 18% GST per additional computer for Professional, or ₹1,000 plus 18% GST for Business. These charges are not included in the displayed installation price.

How will I receive my product key?

Your product key is delivered by email after confirmed payment. You can book a free demo or request a quotation before purchasing.

Terms & conditions

  • Taxes would be applicable, i.e. 18% GST (chargeable on installation of the software).
  • LAN (Multi User) charges Rs. 1000 + 18% GST for per computer for Business.
  • LAN (Multi User) charges Rs. 500 + 18% GST for per computer for Professional.
  • Product key will be delivered through email after confirmed payment.
  • Customer support and software updates will be free till one year from the date of purchase; after that AMC would be applicable.
