How to choose GST software for a CA practice: 8 questions to ask

By Siddharth Enterprises · Published 23 October 2026 · Last updated 23 October 2026 · 4 min read

Use these questions to compare GST software on what matters in daily work. Ask for a trial and test them on your own clients' data.

1. Can it manage many GSTINs from one place?

A practice needs a single view of return status and filing dates across all clients, with search and filters.

2. Can it file directly to the portal?

Look for upload and download from inside the software, with EVC/OTP or DSC support, so that you do not log in to the portal client by client.

3. How good is ITC reconciliation?

Test GSTR-2B reconciliation with a messy purchase register. Check how it handles invoice-number differences and whether IMS actions are supported.

4. Does it cover e-invoice and e-way bill?

If your clients raise e-invoices, bulk generation and an e-way bill from an IRN save a lot of time.

5. What reports do you get?

Good reports include form-wise previews, GSTR-3B vs GSTR-1 comparison, party-wise and rate-wise summaries and Excel exports.

6. Who supports you, and in which language?

Ask where the support team is, the hours they work and whether they help in the language your staff are comfortable with.

7. What does it really cost?

Compare the installation fee, the yearly renewal, charges for extra users and the GST on top. Ask what is included in the first year.

8. Can you try it first?
