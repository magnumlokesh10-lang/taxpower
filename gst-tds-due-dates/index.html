Quick reference: GST

Return or paymentStandard due date
GSTR-1 (monthly)11th of the next month
GSTR-1 (QRMP, quarterly)13th of the month after the quarter
IFF (optional)13th of the next month, for the first two months of a quarter
GSTR-3B (monthly)20th of the next month
GSTR-3B (QRMP, quarterly)22nd or 24th of the month after the quarter, by state. Maharashtra: 22nd
PMT-06 (QRMP)25th of the next month, for the first two months of a quarter
GSTR-7 and GSTR-810th of the next month
GSTR-613th of the next month
CMP-0818th of the month after the quarter
GSTR-430 April after the financial year
GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C31 December after the financial year

Quick reference: TDS and TCS

Return, payment or certificateStandard due date
TDS and TCS deposit7th of the next month; TDS for March by 30 April
TDS statements (Forms 138, 140, 144)31 July, 31 October, 31 January and 31 May
TCS statement (Form 143)Check the Income Tax portal for the current due date
Form 16 (Form 130)15 June after the financial year
Form 16A (Form 131)Usually 15 August, 15 November, 15 February and 15 June

Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026, so TDS and TCS forms and sections have new numbers. Forms 24Q, 26Q, 27Q and 27EQ became Forms 138, 140, 144 and 143. Form 16, 16A and 27D became Forms 130, 131 and 133.

Before you rely on a date

The government often extends due dates, and some extensions apply only to certain states or taxpayers. Always confirm on gst.gov.in and incometax.gov.in. Do not wait for the last day, because the portals are busy near deadlines.
